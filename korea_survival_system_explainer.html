What students don't know when they arrive
Transportation
Korea's transit system is efficient but unfamiliar — T-money cards, express buses, subway transfers, and app-based routes are not obvious to newcomers.
Essential apps
KakaoTalk, Naver Map, Coupang, and Baemin are daily necessities. Most students don't know these exist until weeks in.
Food & dietary needs
Vegetarian, halal, and allergen-aware options require knowing exactly where to look. Korean menus are often only in Korean.
University culture
Korean academic expectations, attendance norms, professor etiquette, and club culture are very different from most home universities.
Korean etiquette
Age-based speech levels, dining customs, public behavior, and unspoken social rules affect daily interaction in ways students don't anticipate.
Daily survival
Banking without a Korean ID, finding a sim card, understanding bank apps, getting a health check — all of this is confusing the first time.
