Critical Thinking 🧠

Reflection Section

This project required critical thinking because the objective was not simply to create a traditional informational website. The design process focused on building an experience that feels useful, interactive, and meaningful for real students.

Usability & Organization

Information was structured carefully to improve clarity, navigation, and overall user understanding.

Real Student Challenges

The project considered practical struggles students experience in daily academic and cultural environments.

Interaction Design

Visual hierarchy, transitions, and interface behavior were designed to feel intuitive and engaging.

Accessibility & Inclusion

Simplicity, readability, and thoughtful design choices were used to support different types of users.

Many parts of this project are intentionally unconventional compared to traditional student work. Instead of focusing only on presenting information, the goal was to create an experience that feels interactive, immersive, and genuinely helpful.

Throughout development, the project involved continuous planning, experimentation, creativity, and problem-solving. Each design decision required balancing aesthetics, functionality, cultural understanding, and practical usefulness in order to create a modern and thoughtfully designed final result.
