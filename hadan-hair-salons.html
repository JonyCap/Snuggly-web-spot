Tips for Foreign Students

Everything you need to know before walking into a salon.

💳
Practical Info
  • Most salons accept both cash and card (debit/credit)
  • Reservations are usually not needed — walk-ins welcome
  • Weekday mornings are the least busy
  • Best to avoid peak hours (evenings and weekends)
  • Staff at Beauty Me are especially comfortable with non-Korean speakers
📸
Pro Tip: Bring a Reference Photo! Show a picture of the hairstyle you want — this is the most effective way to communicate your desired style, even without speaking Korean. Save a photo from Pinterest, Instagram, or Google beforehand.
