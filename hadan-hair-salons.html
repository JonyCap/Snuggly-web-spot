💡 Helpful Tips

Tips for Foreign Students

📸
Bring a Reference Photo
The single most helpful thing you can do! Show a photo of the hairstyle you want. This eliminates language barriers completely and ensures you get exactly what you're after.
💳
Cash & Card Both Accepted
Most salons in Hadan accept both cash and card. Bringing some cash (₩ won) as backup is always a safe idea just in case.
⏰
Timing Tip
Weekday afternoons are usually quietest. Weekends can get busy — if possible, go between Tuesday–Thursday for a more relaxed experience.
😊
Be Patient & Smile
Korean hair stylists are generally very attentive. Even a little effort with Korean phrases will be greatly appreciated and warmly received.

🗣️ Useful Korean Phrases

Copy these or show your phone screen to the stylist

머리 자르고 싶어요
meo-ri ja-reu-go si-peo-yo
I want a haircut
짧게 해주세요
jjalb-ge hae-ju-se-yo
Please cut it short
조금만 잘라주세요
jo-geum-man jal-la-ju-se-yo
Just a little trim, please
얼마예요?
eol-ma-ye-yo?
How much is it?
이 사진처럼 해주세요
i sa-jin-cheo-reom hae-ju-se-yo
Please do it like this photo
감사합니다
gam-sa-hab-ni-da
Thank you
