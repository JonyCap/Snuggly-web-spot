Stay Safe

Safety & Preparation

A great hike starts with good preparation. Follow these guidelines to hike safely in Busan.

💧
Bring Enough Water

Carry at least 1.5L per person. Korean mountains can get hot, especially in summer. Dehydration is the #1 cause of hiking problems.

👟
Wear Proper Shoes

Hiking boots or trail shoes with grip are essential. Avoid sandals, dress shoes, or flat sneakers — Korean mountain paths are rocky and steep.

⛈️
Check Weather First

Rainy days make paths extremely slippery. Avoid hiking during heavy rain, typhoons, or after large rainfalls. Always check forecast before going.

🔋
Bring a Power Bank

Your phone is your map, camera, and emergency tool. Always carry a power bank. Dead phone = no navigation on mountain trails.

🌙
Avoid Hiking Alone at Night

For night hikes (especially Hwangnyeongsan), always go with at least one other person. Bring a headlamp and stick to main trails only.

🧃
Pack Energy Snacks

Kimbap, energy bars, and trail mix are perfect Korean hiking snacks. Low blood sugar makes descents dangerous — keep energy up.

🌡️
Dress in Layers

Mountain weather changes fast. Mornings can be cool, peaks windy, and descents warm. Bring a light jacket even in summer.

🚨
Emergency Number

Korea emergency: 119 (fire/medical) or 112 (police). Mountain rescue in Korea is free and professional — don't hesitate to call.

⚡
Weather Warning System

Busan experiences heavy rain (장마) from June–July and occasional typhoons in August–September. During these periods, many trails close automatically. Always check the official Busan weather forecast at weather.go.kr before any mountain trip.

Plan Your Trip

Best Seasons for Hiking

Busan's mountains transform dramatically through the year — each season offers a completely different experience.

🌸
Spring
March — May
★★★★☆

Cherry blossoms and azaleas paint the mountains pink. Comfortable 15–20°C temperatures make this an ideal hiking season. Trails are lively with students and families.

☀️
Summer
June — August
★★☆☆☆

Lush green forests but intense heat and humidity. Heavy rain season (장마) in June–July. Only recommended for early morning hikes before 9am. Watch for typhoon warnings.

🍂
Autumn
September — November
★★★★★

PEAK SEASON — the absolute best time to hike in Busan. Crimson and gold foliage, crystal-clear air, and perfect 15–22°C temperatures. Book this into your exchange student calendar!

❄️
Winter
December — February
★★★☆☆

Magical snow-dusted peaks and crisp air, but paths become icy and treacherous. Yakchakspikes (ice cleats) are essential. Sunrise and night city views are spectacular in winter.

Capture Busan

Best Photography Spots

These locations will fill your camera roll with shots that don't look like they're from Busan — they look like art.

🌅
Geumjeongsan Summit
Sunrise Panorama

The highest point in Busan offers a 360° sunrise that sweeps from the mountains to the ocean. Arrive 30 minutes before dawn for golden hour light across the fortress walls.

💡 Pro tip: Hike up Friday night and camp for the Saturday sunrise shot
🌊
Igidae Cliffs
Ocean & Rock Drama

The dramatic coastal cliffs where dark rocks meet turquoise sea create natural compositions that look straight from a travel magazine. Blue hour is especially stunning.

💡 Best shots: 30 min before and after sunset facing west
🌃
Hwangnyeongsan Night View
City Lights Skyline

Busan's most iconic night photography location. The entire city spreads below like a circuit board. Long-exposure shots capture light trails from the highway interchanges beneath.

💡 Bring a tripod — 3s exposures at ISO 800 give perfect results
🏙️
Jangsan Skyline
Haeundae to Gwangalli

On clear days you can see Haeundae Beach, Gwangalli Bridge, and the Busan skyline in one frame. The afternoon haze softens the scene into watercolor-like layers.

💡 Best clarity: Early morning after rain when air is cleanest
